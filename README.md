## Engineering Approach

- **Business-first:** Start with the operational problem and required outcome.
- **Evidence-first:** Use architecture, traces, tests, and real system behavior to support technical claims.
- **Production-focused:** Consider reliability, fallbacks, security, observability, and maintainability from the beginning.
- **Human-centered:** Build AI to support people and strengthen teams—not to hide limitations or remove necessary human judgment.
- **Direct ownership:** Remain involved from architecture and development through deployment and improvement.
